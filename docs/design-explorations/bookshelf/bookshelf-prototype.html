Bookshelf study

Every book is drawn from its data: title, author, colour and kind (cloth, leather, jacket or paperback). Height, thickness, lettering and wear follow from the title, so a book always looks the same.

Hover a book to ease it off the shelf. Choose one and it turns face out with its note. Arrow keys move along a shelf; Escape puts the book back.

All titles, authors and notes are invented samples, not Susana’s books.
