## Design Context

### Users
Two audiences, weighted equally:
- **Hiring managers** in product and music technology deciding whether to talk to Susana. They scan: what does she work on, what has she done, how do I reach her.
- **Peers and collaborators** in music education, music licensing and product. They read: how does she think, what does she care about, what is she reading and listening to.
Mostly laptops, often arriving from a link; phones are a close second.

### Brand Personality
**Precise, playful, intimate**, and above all **tasteful and quiet**: the room is the statement.

### Aesthetic Direction
- The room is the heart of the homepage: a cinematic 1.9:1 frame with softly rounded corners on a near-black, warm-tinted page.
- Homepage contains: a slim editorial masthead above the room (Susana's name and disciplines, a quiet About · Work · Contact menu, and the scene control for time of day and weather at the far right), the room itself (its objects are the main way in), one low liquid-glass player bar centred beneath it (miniature artwork, title, play/pause; it pauses the room's motion and, once connected, shows what she's listening to on Spotify), a small copyright on the right, and a first-visit "Explore the room" chip that disappears after the first interaction.
- Interactive objects (record player, books, laptop, desk books, the woman, the coffee mug, the lamp, the cat) carry a barely visible amber halo traced from their silhouettes; hover warms it, grows the object about 4% from its base, and shows a small tooltip.
- Panels are uncoated off-white card with near-black ink and **one spot colour**: the vermilion of the record label in the illustration (`--spot`). The spot colour is rare: current tab, small numbers, focus, progress.
- Type (chosen by Susana): **Newsreader** for the name, headings, ledes and book spines, **DM Sans** for navigation, body paragraphs and controls. Upright only, no italics. Sentence case everywhere; no tracked capitals.
- The Bookshelf is a real shelf: spines in colours taken from the room, standing on boards.
- Anti-references (user feedback): boxed outline buttons, monogram badges, floating dev widgets, quote-style italic taglines with a side rule, serif-italic "tasteful AI" type (Ibarra/Host Grotesk were rejected), tracklists/"Side A" gimmicks on the homepage, wide-set uppercase labels, condensed display grotesks, anything "unnecessary".

### Design Principles
1. **The room is the cover.** Everything else is quiet typography around it.
2. **Less on the page.** If it isn't the room, the name, navigation or the player, it lives inside a panel or not at all.
3. **One spot colour, used rarely.**
4. **Nothing invented.** Empty content stays hidden on the live site; dev-only notes explain what to add.
5. **Calm motion, exact alignment.**
